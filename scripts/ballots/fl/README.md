# Florida county official ballots (Nov 3, 2026)

## Palm Beach County (v53)
- Ballots: Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections, "2026 General Election Sample Ballots" (votepalmbeach.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2735): one printed 2-page ballot per precinct, 817 ballots (812 geographic precincts + 5 non-geographic 9901.x/9902 ballots, which are not used). The site needs a real browser (browser_download.mjs).
- Map: the Supervisor of Elections' "Precincts_2026" layer (services1.arcgis.com/ZWOoUZbtaYePLlPw PRECINCTS/FeatureServer/0, used in its Voting Precinct and District Map), 813 polygons. 812 match the ballots 1:1, plus 1 "NP" area.
- Parse: palmbeach_parse.py. Check on every ballot: every printed oval is an option of a contest (45,196 ovals, 0 left over); 0 ballots flagged.
- Contests: 31 races + 12 questions (6 judicial retentions, Amendments 1-3, County Parks / Library District / School District questions). Every race matches the Supervisor's "All Contests" sample ballot (candidates, order, write-in lines).
- Districts: the Congress, State Senate, State House and School Board numbers printed on each precinct's ballot equal the Supervisor's own district layers at an interior point of the precinct: CD 812/812, HD 812/812, SD 622/622 (where on the ballot), School Board 129/129. 0 mismatches.

## Orange County (v54)
- Ballots: Orange County Supervisor of Elections, "2026 General Election by Precinct" (voteorangefl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Gen26CombinedBallots.pdf, sha256 6f930af9b7893800…): 660 pages, 330 printed 2-page English/Spanish ballots for 263 precincts (59 precincts have 2+ ballots).
- Parse: orange_parse.py. Every printed oval is an option of a contest (13,658 ovals, 0 left over); 0 ballots flagged. 34 distinct contests; each contest is printed identically on every ballot that has it; every candidate name appears on the Supervisor's composite ballot (26GEN113-CB-EN). Amendment 1-3 and Muñiz retention texts are identical to Palm Beach's printed text.
- Map: the Supervisor's "Precincts" layer (OCSOE_Precincts, updated 2026-09-21, 263 polygons = the 263 ballot precincts exactly), cut by its "2026 Orange County Congressional Districts", "Commission Districts November 2026", "2026 Sub Precincts" (State House/Senate, Soil & Water, School Board) and Special Districts (Vista Lakes CDD) layers: orange_overlay.py. In every precinct every part of at least 1% of its area matches exactly one of that precinct's printed ballots by the districts printed on it (uncontested races are not printed in Florida); all 330 ballots are placed. Parts that match no ballot (≤1% slivers, plus 7.5% of precinct 101 and 3.8% of 611 where the sub-precinct layer shows another House district) are left off the map, so an address there is labelled + blue button.
- Independent check: 80 random interior points, Census TIGERweb 2024 State Senate + House: 160/160 agree with the ballot (71 contested matches, 89 uncontested correctly absent).
