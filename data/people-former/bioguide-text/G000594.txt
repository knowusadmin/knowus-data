GONZALES, Tony, a Representative from Texas; born on October 10, 1980, in Anniston, Ala.; attended Thomas Edison High School, San Antonio, Tex.; A.A., Chaminade University, Honolulu, Hawaii; B.S., Excelsior College, Albany, N.Y.; attended Georgetown University, Washington, D.C.; M.A., American Public University, Charles Town, W.Va.; attended University of Southern Mississippi, Hattiesburg, Miss.; United States Navy, 1999-2019; faculty, Pensacola State College, Pensacola, Fla.; faculty, University of Maryland, College Park, Md.; community representative, Head Start policy council, San Antonio, Tex., 2018-2019; legislative fellow, Office of Legislative Affairs, United States Navy, 2016-2017; elected as a Republican to the One Hundred Seventeenth and to the two succeeding Congresses until his resignation on April 14, 2026 (January 3, 2021-April 14, 2026).
