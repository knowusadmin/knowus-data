MOYLAN, James, a Delegate from Guam; born on July 18, 1962; graduated from John F. Kennedy High School, Tumon, Guam, 1981; B.S., University of Guam, Mangilao, Guam; United States Army; parole officer; business owner; member of the Guam territorial senate, 2019-2023; elected as a Republican to the One Hundred Eighteenth and to the succeeding Congress (January 3, 2023-present).
