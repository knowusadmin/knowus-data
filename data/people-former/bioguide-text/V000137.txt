VANCE, James David (J.D.), a Senator from Ohio and a Vice President of the United States; born in Middletown, Ohio, August 2, 1984; graduated Middletown High School, 2003; B.A., political science and philosophy, Ohio State University, 2009; J.D., Yale Law School, 2013; United States Marine Corps, 2003-2007; served in Operation Iraqi Freedom; lawyer; venture capitalist; writer; lecturer; non-profit organization founder; elected as a Republican to the United States Senate in 2022 and served from January 3, 2023, until January 10, 2025, when he resigned to become Vice President; elected Vice President of the United States on the Republican ticket headed by Donald J. Trump in 2024.
