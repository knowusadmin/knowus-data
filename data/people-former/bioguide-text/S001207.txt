SHERRILL, Mikie, a Representative from New Jersey; born in Alexandria, Va., January 19, 1972; graduated from South Lakes High School, Reston, Va., 1990; B.S., United States Naval Academy, Annapolis, Md., 1994; M.Sc., London School of Economics and Political Science, London, England, 2003; J.D., Georgetown University, Washington, D.C., 2007; United States Navy, 1994-2003; lawyer; staff, United States Attorney for the District of New Jersey, 2012-2015; Assistant United States Attorney for the District of New Jersey, 2015-2016; elected as a Democrat to the One Hundred Sixteenth and to the three succeeding Congresses until her resignation on November 20, 2025 (January 3, 2019-November 20, 2025); Governor of New Jersey, 2026-present.
