HERNÁNDEZ, Pablo José, a Resident Commissioner from Puerto Rico; born in San Juan, P.R., May 11, 1991; A.B., Harvard College, Cambridge, Mass., 2013; J.D., Stanford University, Stanford, Calif., 2017; lawyer, private practice; clerk, U.S. District Court of Puerto Rico; author; elected as a Popular Democrat to the One Hundred Nineteenth Congress to a four-year term in 2024 (January 3, 2025-present); caucused with the Democratic Party.
