GREENE, Marjorie Taylor, a Representative from Georgia; born Milledgeville, Baldwin County, Ga., May 27, 1974; graduated from South Forsyth High School, Cumming, Ga., 1992; B.B.A., University of Georgia, Athens, Ga., 1996; business owner; elected as a Republican to the One Hundred Seventeenth and to the two succeeding Congresses until her resignation on January 5, 2026 (January 3, 2021-January 5, 2026); one of the managers appointed by the House of Representatives in 2024 to conduct the impeachment proceedings of Alejandro N. Mayorkas, United States Secretary of Homeland Security.
